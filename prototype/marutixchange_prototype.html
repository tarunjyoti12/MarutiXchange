Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying and selling on MarutiXchange.

BuyingHow do I know if a listed car is genuine?
Every listing on MarutiXchange goes through a multi-step verification process. Sellers complete KYC with a government-issued ID. Cars undergo a 200-point inspection by certified technicians covering engine, electrical, body and interior. You'll see a ✓ Verified badge on all trusted listings. Additionally, our automated system flags any suspicious pricing or duplicate seller activity before a listing goes live.
BuyingCan I take a test drive before buying?
Yes, absolutely. Every listing has a "Test Drive" button. Click it, fill in your name, city, preferred date and time slot — the seller receives your request and typically confirms within 24 hours. Test drives are completely free and non-obligatory. You can also request multiple test drives before making your decision.
BuyingWhat does the auction feature work? How do I bid?
Navigate to Live Bids in the top navigation. You'll see all cars currently up for auction with real-time countdown timers. Sign in, click on any car, enter your bid amount (must be higher than the current highest bid), and confirm. The highest bidder when the timer ends wins the car. Alternatively, use the Buy Now price to purchase instantly without bidding.
SellingHow do I list my Maruti car for sale?
Click "List Your Car" in the navigation or scroll to the Sell section on the Home page. The process takes 5 simple steps: (1) Enter car info — model, year, variant, fuel type; (2) Upload photos; (3) Set your asking price; (4) Configure test drive availability; (5) Add your contact details. Your listing goes live within minutes after our quick verification check.
SellingDoes MarutiXchange charge any commission or listing fees?
MarutiXchange is a zero-commission C2C platform. Listing your car is completely free. There are no hidden charges when you sell. The full transaction amount goes directly to you. We make our revenue through premium listing boosts and value-added services like RC transfer assistance and inspection reports — none of which are mandatory.
DocumentsWho handles the RC transfer and ownership paperwork?
Our RC Transfer Support team handles the entire documentation process end-to-end — including Form 29/30, NOC from the seller's RTO, insurance transfer, and the new RC in the buyer's name. The process typically takes 7–10 working days. You'll receive real-time status updates via SMS and email throughout.
PaymentIs it safe to pay on MarutiXchange? How does the payment work?
Payments are processed through our secure escrow system. Your payment is held safely until you physically take delivery and confirm receipt of the car. Only then is the amount released to the seller. This protects both parties. We support NEFT, RTGS, UPI, and bank transfer. Cash transactions are strongly discouraged for your security.
GeneralIs MarutiXchange only for Maruti Suzuki cars?
Yes — MarutiXchange is India's only dedicated Maruti Suzuki resale marketplace. By focusing exclusively on Maruti cars across both Arena and Nexa segments, we provide deeper expertise, more accurate pricing benchmarks, and a buyer community that specifically wants Maruti vehicles. This focus means faster sales and better prices for sellers compared to generic multi-brand platforms.
